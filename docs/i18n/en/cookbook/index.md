---
title: Cookbook
description: Small, runnable Loxia programs for common tasks.
---

The cookbook collects complete programs, not fragments. Each recipe is based on a file in
`exemplos/` and is kept runnable by the documentation tests.

The first recipes are being written:

- Fibonacci, iteration and recursion.
- Classes, inheritance and `super`.
- Quicksort with lists and pattern matching.

Until then, the [examples directory](https://github.com/loxia-lang/loxia/tree/main/exemplos)
has the complete programs the recipes are based on.
