title: Learn Loxia in 30 minutes
description: A guided tour of values, control flow, functions, collections and classes.
---

This tour is for programmers who already know another language. It moves fast, but every
complete snippet is runnable and is checked by the documentation tests.

1. [Install and run](install/) — the REPL, a script and `--check`.
2. [Values and bindings](values/) — `val`, `var`, `Int`, `Float`, `String`, `Bool`, `nil`.
3. [Control flow](control-flow/) — `if`, `while`, `for-in`, `elif` and ranges.
4. [Functions and closures](functions/) — `fn`, lambdas, defaults and variadics.
5. [Collections](collections/) — `List[T]`, `Map[K, V]` and tuples.
6. [Classes, enums and match](classes/) — objects, inheritance and exhaustive `match`.
7. [Errors and modules](errors-modules/) — `try`/`except`, `raise` and `import`.
8. [Next steps](next-steps/) — the standard library, the native backend and the reference.

The normative details live in the [reference](../reference/); this tour teaches the shape
of the language, not every corner.
